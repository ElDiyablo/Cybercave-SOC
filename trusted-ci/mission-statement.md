## Primary Mission Statement

The Cybercave Security Operations Center (SOC) exists to defend Murray
State University's digital infrastructure, advance the university's
academic mission through applied cybersecurity practice, and develop the
next generation of security professionals from within the Murray State
student body.

We operate with integrity, transparency, and a commitment to continuous
learning — protecting our campus while building skills that translate
directly to careers in security and that support the broader security
posture of Murray State University.

---

## Mission Pillars

### 1. Defend
Provide real, continuous security monitoring and incident response for
Murray State's campus network infrastructure. The SOC serves as an
additional layer of human-driven detection and response on top of existing
automated controls — focused specifically on the Palo Alto firewall
telemetry that flows through the university cyber range.

### 2. Develop
Give Murray State students hands-on experience with enterprise-grade
security tools, frameworks, and processes that cannot be replicated in
a classroom environment alone. Every shift worked, every ticket written,
and every investigation completed builds documented, portfolio-ready
experience directly applicable to security careers.

### 3. Support Academic Security
Recognize that Murray State's academic mission depends on the integrity,
confidentiality, and availability of university data and systems. The SOC
contributes to the protection of campus infrastructure by monitoring
network traffic, identifying anomalous behavior, and escalating potential
threats that could affect university operations or data integrity.

### 4. Serve the Community
Share knowledge, open-source tools, and security awareness resources
with the broader Murray State campus and local community. Operate
transparently so that other student-led security programs can learn
from and build upon the Cybercave SOC model.

---

## Scope

The Cybercave SOC is authorized to monitor and respond to security
events within the following scope:

**In Scope:**
- Network traffic logs from the Murray State cyber range Palo Alto NGFW
- ELK SIEM alerts and dashboards generated from in-scope log sources
- Threat intelligence enrichment for indicators observed in in-scope logs
- Security awareness resources published to the SOC GitHub Pages site
- Incident tickets submitted by analysts during authorized shifts

**Out of Scope:**
- Production university enterprise systems not connected to the cyber range
- Student, faculty, or staff personal devices
- Any system not explicitly authorized by the faculty advisor
- Active exploitation or penetration testing of any system
- Access to university data systems beyond what is required for monitoring
